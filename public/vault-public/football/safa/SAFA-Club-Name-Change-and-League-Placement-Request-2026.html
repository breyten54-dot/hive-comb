RE: NOTIFICATION AND REQUEST FOR APPROVAL — CLUB NAME CHANGES AND LEAGUE PLACEMENT (2026 SEASON)

Dear Sir / Madam,

We write on behalf of the two affiliated clubs identified above to formally notify the South African Football Association (SAFA) of, and to respectfully request approval for, changes to the registered names and league placement of the clubs, with effect from the 2026 season.

We request that SAFA note and approve the following reciprocal changes:

  1. (a) The club currently registered as Manning Rangers Sporting (SAFA affiliation / registration no. [__________]) will change its registered name to Ethekwini City F.C. and will compete out of the Super League.
  2. (b) The club currently registered as Ethekwini City F.C. (SAFA affiliation / registration no. [__________]) will change its registered name to Manning Rangers Sporting and will compete out of the Zonal League.

For the avoidance of doubt, the two changes constitute a reciprocal exchange of club names: the name "Ethekwini City F.C." and the name "Manning Rangers Sporting" are being swapped between the two existing, separately registered clubs, together with their respective league placements as set out above. We ask that SAFA's registration records be updated accordingly so that each club's registration, playing history and registered players are correctly reflected under its new name, and to avoid any confusion between the two clubs going forward.

These changes have been resolved by the management committee of each club in accordance with the club's constitution. We are able to provide any supporting documentation SAFA may require, including the resolutions and minutes of each club, the club constitutions, and any updated registration or affiliation forms.

We kindly request written confirmation from SAFA once the records have been updated, together with any forms or steps still required from us to give effect to these changes. We are happy to provide further information or to meet with the relevant SAFA structure should that assist.

We thank you for your assistance and look forward to your confirmation.
